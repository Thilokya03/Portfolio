MERCon 2026 — Accepted

Forecasting Weather-Driven Price Dynamics Across Sri Lankan Tea Market Catalogues

Research | Data Science | Machine Learning | Time Series Forecasting

A collaborative research project investigating weather-driven and market-related factors affecting Sri Lankan tea auction prices. The workflow transforms real-world semi-structured auction reports into analysis-ready datasets, integrates production, catalogue, and weather information, and supports exploratory, causal, and predictive analysis.

The research has been accepted to MERCon 2026 and demonstrates an end-to-end workflow from real-world data extraction and feature engineering to time-aware machine learning and forecasting.

Key Contributions

  • Tea auction report and PDF data processing
  • Weather and market data integration
  • Data cleaning and feature engineering
  • Exploratory and time-series analysis
  • Causality and predictive modeling workflow
  • Reproducible collaborative research pipeline

Published Dataset

Sri Lanka Holiday Dataset

Data Engineering | Public Dataset | Calendar & Holiday Analytics

A publicly released Sri Lankan holiday and calendar dataset created by collecting, cleaning, validating, and integrating holiday information from public sources. The dataset is designed to support data science, forecasting, time-series analysis, and other applications that require structured Sri Lankan calendar features.

Published on Hugging Face as a reusable public data resource for research, analytics, machine learning, and forecasting workflows.

Key Contributions

  • Structured Sri Lankan holiday and calendar data
  • Automated data collection and processing workflows
  • Data cleaning, normalization, and validation
  • Analysis-ready calendar features
  • Reusable public dataset for research and forecasting
