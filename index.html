Research Project

Sri Lankan Tea Auction Price Analysis and Predictive Research Workflow

Research | Data Science | Machine Learning | Collaborative Project

A collaborative research project focused on analyzing Sri Lankan tea auction price behavior using auction reports, production data, market segment information, and weather-related variables. The project builds a reproducible data pipeline to convert real-world semi-structured tea auction reports into clean analytical datasets for exploratory analysis and predictive modeling.

This project demonstrates my ability to transform real-world semi-structured data into research-ready datasets and apply data science methods to analyze commodity market behavior.

Key Features

  • Tea auction PDF/report data processing
  • Data cleaning and preprocessing
  • Feature engineering and market segmentation
  • Exploratory data analysis
  • Baseline modeling preparation
  • Reproducible research workflow
